A few things
before you download.

Can I add notes to individual numbers?

Yes. Add notes to a number or an entire expression to explain a price, quantity, discount, or delivery charge.

Can I save and search calculation history?

Yes. Save calculation pages, search titles and notes, and reopen existing records to continue a calculation.

Does it work offline?

Calculations, notes, and local history work offline. Network features, such as sending feedback, require an internet connection.

Which operations are supported?

Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, parentheses, percentages, and live result previews. You can adjust decimal places, thousands separators, and keyboard colors.

Are my calculations uploaded?

In the current released version, expressions, results, and notes stay on your device. Usage analytics and feedback you choose to submit are handled as described in the privacy policy.
