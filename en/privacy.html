Privacy Policy

Updated: September 26, 2026

Calculation content stays on your device

Expressions, results, calculation history, and note contents remain on your device and are never uploaded as usage analytics. The calculator works offline.

Usage analytics

Usage analytics is enabled by default. When the analytics service is configured, the app sends a random installation ID, session IDs, event times, usage counts, foreground duration, feature counts, app version, device model, OS version, language, locale-region preference, and timezone. The installation ID links activity within one installation; it is not a real-world identity.

Country and region

The service infers country/region from the connection IP and stores only the country code, not the raw IP. GPS permission is not requested. VPNs and proxies can affect the result. Locale preferences are kept separate from inferred location.

Purpose and retention

Analytics helps improve the app by measuring activity, frequency, and retention. Online events are kept for 30 days, session summaries for 90 days, and daily aggregates for 13 months. Installation metadata is removed after 13 months of inactivity. Local backups rotate after 7 days. Unsent events are kept on-device for up to 7 days, capped at 1000 records.

Data requests

Contact us for questions or requests to remove related analytics records. Uninstalling the app does not automatically erase previously received server data, which is handled according to the retention periods above.

Security and third parties

Production analytics is sent over HTTPS to a service operated by the developer. The app includes no third-party advertising or analytics SDK, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not use this data for cross-app advertising tracking. The dashboard is restricted to authorized administrators.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. Contact the developer if you believe we received data relating to a child.

Updates and contact

This policy is updated to reflect actual data practices. For privacy questions or data requests, contact flynnyoungtop@qq.com.
